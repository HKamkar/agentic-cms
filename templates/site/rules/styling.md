# Styling — read `STANDARD.md` before writing markup or CSS; these are the invariants

- Utilities for layout, spacing, type, colour, visibility and hover/focus; a `<Name>.module.css` next to the component only for what utilities cannot say (layered or gradient backgrounds, keyframes, backdrop filters, a static transform on an animated element). Plain CSS, no `@apply`, breakpoints spelled out, joined to utilities with `cx()`.
- Tokens only in the `@theme static` block of `src/app/globals.css`; per-breakpoint values in `src/styles/base.css`. A component asks for a colour, a radius or a shadow by its role where the site has one (`panel`, `line`, `on-ink`, `rounded-panel`, `rounded-control`, `shadow-panel`, `shadow-glow` on a site from `init` since 0.8.0, or one that added them) and names a value only where no role does; a new role is a token first, and a colour becomes a token at its second use. The type scale is the site's (`text-h1`…`text-h6`, `text-body`), never `text-base` / `text-sm`. One colour utility per property per element.
- The site is designed, looked at and measured at its widths — the profile's, `widths` in `src/config/harness.ts`; for a new site four: 390, 768, 1440 and 1920. Nothing that moves widens the page at any of them.
- Preflight zeroes margins, paddings, borders and list markers: never `m-0`, `p-0`, `list-none`. Decorative elements are real `aria-hidden` elements, not pseudo-elements. Interactive things are real `<button>`s and links; never `outline-none` on anything a keyboard reaches.
- Motion is the layer the site has (its `STANDARD.md` §7): a motion folder of the site's own while it keeps one, otherwise `agentic-cms/ix` — the play-once layer where the layout renders `motionScript()`, and for a site's first motion; the reveals that replay (`Fx`, `OnView`) on a site that moves with them and has not adopted the play-once layer, and elsewhere only where a replay on re-entry is wanted — with its start states and keyframes in `src/styles/motion.css`. No `translate-*` / `rotate-*` / `scale-*` utility sits on an element a motion layer moves.
- Where the layout renders `motionScript()`, the play-once layer (`Reveal`, `RevealGroup`, `Sequence`, `part()`): a root plays once, when it first enters the viewport, never on a hover or a second scroll; loops run only on screen; nothing is hidden unless `motionScript()` armed the page; the first screen's copy uses `intro`; curves and durations are the `--ease-*` / `--motion-*` tokens; a section's motion is its parts, never a transition or animation class; a sequence longer than two seconds says `settle`.
- Where the site uses them, the reveals that replay on re-entry (`Fx`, `OnView`): animation targets are `data-ix` attributes through `ix(name)`, never a styling class; every animated component checks `useReducedMotionPref()`; a sequence longer than two seconds declares `data-settle` on its section.
- Images stay `<img>` with `width`, `height` and `alt`; above the fold in a server component `EagerImage` (`agentic-cms/components`), everything else `loading="lazy"`; assets under `public/images/<page>/` or `public/images/ui/`.
- **The preferred tier warns, and fails nothing** (`node_modules/agentic-cms/docs/primitives.md` § Enforced): `agentic-cms/styling`, an arbitrary value or property, an arbitrary breakpoint or a style colour outside the tokens in `ui/`, the motion layer and `src/app` (a section or a pattern fails them); `agentic-cms/motion`, an animation library but the site's motion layer, a `requestAnimationFrame` loop, an `IntersectionObserver`, `el.animate()`, a transition or animation class or style outside the motion layer, and (the guard's `transitions`) one declared in CSS outside it and `src/styles/motion.css`; `agentic-cms/images`, `next/image` or an eager `<img>` in a server component; `agentic-cms/icons`, an `<svg>` outside `Icon`; `agentic-cms/graphics`, a rendering library or a `<canvas>` (graphics ship pre-rendered from the lab). The kit's part comes first; a file that keeps its own says why in one line, an `allow` entry in `src/config/components.json` (`{ "rule", "files", "reason" }`) or an inline disable with `-- <why>`, and the warning goes quiet. A warning with no reason stays in the lint's output, for the review.
- Any change that must not move a pixel proves it, once built: `pnpm kit visual-parity proof` — the baseline of develop, the tree and the compare, at the profile's widths and in each scheme it names (a site without a profile writes one first: without it a proof is eight widths in light) — clean (`--motion` when animation code, targets or transitions change; `--states` when hover / focus / checked / open change). A design change is its own commit and says so.
- **Tier:** the tokens, motion (`agentic-cms/ix`), images (`EagerImage`, `Media`) and icons (`Icon`) are preferred: in a section or a pattern `no-restricted-syntax`, `react/forbid-elements` and `no-restricted-imports` fail `pnpm lint`, with the guard's `styles` and `keyframes` passes; elsewhere in `src/` a bypass warns, naming the kit's part (`agentic-cms/styling`, `agentic-cms/motion`, `agentic-cms/images`, `agentic-cms/icons`; the guard's `transitions`), and one line of reason quiets it (`allow` in `src/config/components.json`, or an inline disable with its description). <!-- required -->
