# Styling — read `STANDARD.md` before writing markup or CSS; these are the invariants

- Utilities for layout, spacing, type, colour, visibility and hover/focus; a `<Name>.module.css` only for what utilities cannot say — today only `src/components/blog/PostBody.module.css` (the rich-text element rules under `.prose` and the FAQ block). Plain CSS, no `@apply`, breakpoints spelled out (`@media (width < 768px)`), joined to utilities with `cx()`.
- Tokens only in the `@theme static` block of `src/app/globals.css`; the two breakpoint steps of the scale in `src/styles/base.css` (`:root`). Four colour values, each a `light-dark()` pair — `paper`, `ink`, `fill`, `muted` — and role tokens that alias them (`panel`, `line`, `on-ink`); a component uses the role where one exists and a value only where no role does (`border-line` for every hairline, never `border-ink`), a new value or role is a token first, and nothing else exists as a colour utility (never `bg-white`, `text-black`, `border-gray-*`). Radius and shadow exist only as role tokens (`--radius-panel`, `--radius-control`, `--shadow-panel`, `--shadow-glow`) held at the wireframe's `0` / none; no other radius, shadow, blur, gradient, transition or animation utility exists. The type scale is `text-h1`…`text-h6` and `text-body` (never `text-base`/`text-sm`), each heading step with its `--text-hN--letter-spacing`; the fonts are `font-sans` (body and headings alike) and `font-label` (mono, for tags and chips), a label's letter-spacing `--tracking-label`.
- Breakpoints: `max-sm` < 480, `max-md` < 768, `max-lg` < 992, `lg` ≥ 992, `xl` ≥ 1280, `2xl` ≥ 1440, `3xl` ≥ 1920. Rhythm: `py-section`, `max-w-page`, `px-4`, the 4 px spacing scale. Design work is looked at and measured at four widths, 390, 768, 1440 and 1920 — what a new site starts with.
- The theme is `color-scheme: light dark` on `:root`, overridden by `data-theme="light" | "dark"` on `<html>` (the toggle, `localStorage.theme`, the inline script in the layout). A component never reads the theme; it uses the semantic colours.
- Every section renders inside `ui/Section`: `type` is its YAML section type and is also its `id` and `data-section`, and the box's tag is shown on purpose. Cards are a `<ul>` of `ui/Panel` frames (`<Panel as="li">`) with `<h3>` titles after the section's `<h2>` (the audit fails an `<h3>` before the first `<h2>`); a `statement` field is the section's `<h2>`. Shared strings: a card `<Panel as="li" className="flex flex-col gap-2 p-4">`, its title `<Heading as="h3">`, a card grid `grid gap-4 lg:grid-cols-N`, a split `grid gap-4 lg:grid-cols-2` (uneven: the tokens `lg:grid-cols-2-1`, `lg:grid-cols-1-2-1`), a content image `<Media>` (`block h-auto w-full border border-line`), an icon `<Media size="icon">` (`size-16 border border-line`), a statistic `text-h1 font-semibold`, secondary text `text-muted`. Illustrations are `ui/Placeholder`; content pictures are `ui/Media`, never an `<img>` in a section (the component rules, `.claude/rules/components.md`).
- No motion in the wireframe: no `Fx`/`OnView`/`Reveal`/`Sequence` in a section, no `data-ix` or `data-part`, no transitions; nothing hides until JavaScript runs. `agentic-cms/ix` and `src/styles/motion.css` stay for a fork that adds motion, in two layers (`STANDARD.md` §7 says which to choose) — then the play-once layer plays a root once, never on a hover or a second scroll, with its loops only on screen, nothing hidden unless `motionScript()` armed the page and its timings from the `--ease-*` / `--motion-*` tokens; `Fx` targets are `data-ix` through `ix(name)`, never a styling class, and every animated component checks `useReducedMotionPref()`; start states and keyframes live in `motion.css`, and no `translate-*` / `rotate-*` / `scale-*` utility sits on an element either layer moves.
- Preflight zeroes margins, paddings, borders and list markers: never `m-0`, `p-0`, `list-none`. Decorative elements are real `aria-hidden` elements, not pseudo-elements. Interactive things are real `<button>`s and links; never `outline-none` on anything a keyboard reaches (`<main>`, focused by the skip link, is the one exception). One colour utility per property per element.
- Images stay `<img>` with `width`, `height` and `alt`; above the fold in a server component `EagerImage` (`agentic-cms/components`), everything else `loading="lazy"`. `img { height: revert-layer }` means a `w-full` image carries `h-auto`. Placeholders come from `pnpm kit placeholder <out> <w> <h>`; assets live under `public/images/<page>/`, `public/images/ui/`, `public/images/brand/logo.svg`, `public/images/blog/<slug>/`, `public/images/authors/`, and a page's OG image is `public/images/<path-with-hyphens>-og.jpg` (`sections-about-og.jpg` for `/sections/about`).
- **The preferred tier warns, and fails nothing** (`agentic-cms/eslint`, `docs/primitives.md` § Enforced): `agentic-cms/styling`, an arbitrary value or property, an arbitrary breakpoint or a style colour outside the tokens in `ui/`, the motion layer and `src/app` (a section or a pattern fails them); `agentic-cms/motion`, an animation library but `agentic-cms/ix`, a `requestAnimationFrame` loop, an `IntersectionObserver`, `el.animate()`, a transition or animation class or style outside the motion layer, and (the guard's `transitions`) one declared in CSS outside it and `motion.css`; `agentic-cms/images`, `next/image` or an eager `<img>` in a server component; `agentic-cms/icons`, an `<svg>` outside `Icon`; `agentic-cms/graphics`, a rendering library or a `<canvas>`. The kit's part comes first; a file that keeps its own says why in one line, an `allow` entry in `src/config/components.json` (`{ "rule", "files", "reason" }`) or an inline disable with `-- <why>`, and the warning goes quiet. The example keeps two: `Placeholder`'s crossed `<svg>` and the form's off-screen honeypot. A warning with no reason stays in the lint's output, for the review.
- Any change that must not move a pixel: `pnpm kit visual-parity proof` (or `capture <before>` / `<after>` and `compare`) clean, in each scheme the profile names (`src/config/harness.ts`; the example's two); `--motion` when the motion library is in use, `--states` when hover / focus / checked / open change. A design change is its own commit and says so.
- **Tier:** the tokens, motion (`agentic-cms/ix`), images (`EagerImage`, `Media`) and icons (`Icon`) are preferred: in a section or a pattern `no-restricted-syntax`, `react/forbid-elements` and `no-restricted-imports` fail `pnpm lint`, with the guard's `styles` and `keyframes` passes; elsewhere in `src/` a bypass warns, naming the kit's part (`agentic-cms/styling`, `agentic-cms/motion`, `agentic-cms/images`, `agentic-cms/icons`; the guard's `transitions`), and one line of reason quiets it (`allow` in `src/config/components.json`, or an inline disable with its description).
