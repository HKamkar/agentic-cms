The life of one page

Every site begins in the dark

From loose words to the first reader

0106

The open sea

Notes, voice memos, half sentences. All of it is yours, and all of it drifts.

The open sea

The words are already there They just have no shore

They sit in inboxes, chats and old slides, passing by every day without ever becoming a page.

0206

The storm

Every tool keeps its own copy, and every copy changes a little.

The storm

Copies multiply The truth gets harder to find

One price in the brochure, another on the site, a third in the chat. Nobody can say which one is right.

0306

The spine

One file for each page. Every part has its place, and the light follows the order.

The spine

Give every page one place to live

A title, the words, a picture and a date, kept together in one file with its whole history.

0406

The gate

Nothing passes without being read. What fails is held, and the reason is written down.

The gate

A mistake stops here before a reader meets it

Each link, picture and sentence is checked on every build, the same way every time.

0506

The tree

What passed takes root. The light climbs, the branches follow, and every page finds its place.

The tree

One root Every page in its season

Home, guides, stories and answers grow from the same source and never lose track of it.

0606

First light

Every page is ready before anyone asks for it.

First light

Let your site see the light

Built ahead of time, and open the moment someone arrives.
